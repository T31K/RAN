# SEALED - Odyssey Rift surprises

T31K asked for three surprises he would not be told about. Do not open this if you are him.

1. **Golden Soul.** From leg 3, one leg in eight hides a golden soul in the horde (chat hints
   "...something golden flickers among the dead."). Killing it gives every voyager +1500
   Essence. (`m_nGolden`, tier 9 in `GLOdysseyRift.cpp`)
2. **Ithaca.** Typing `ithaca` while standing on the Nexus between legs (leg 1+), once per
   voyage: Athena gives the whole squad +1000 Essence and full HP/MP/SP.
   (`m_bSecretIthaca`)
3. **The Muse.** Survive a leg from leg 7 onward with nobody falling: the Muse sings, every
   voyager's skill tier rises one step for free and gets +777 Essence. Once per voyage.
   (`m_bMuseSang`, `m_bRoundDeath`)
